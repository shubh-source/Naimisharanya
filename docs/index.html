Sacred Tirthas & Ancient Temples

Every inch of Naimisharanya is sanctified with cosmic energy. Explore the paramount shrines and historical landmarks sanctified by the Gods.

Center of Creation
चक्र तीर्थ (Chakra Tirtha)

The Celestial Wheel Kund

When Rishis sought the holiest place for penance, Lord Brahma rolled His mind-borne Manomaya Chakra. Its rim struck here, creating this unfathomable circular pool. A holy dip (Snan) washes away lifetimes of sins.

Open: 04:00 AM - 09:30 PM Sacred Snan Ghat
51 Maha Shaktipeeth
माँ ललिता देवी शक्तिपीठ (Maa Lalita Devi)

Presiding Goddess of Naimish

Revered as one of the supreme 51 Shaktipeeths where the sacred heart of Devi Sati manifested. The divine Akhand Jyoti burns eternally here, granting devotees courage, spiritual liberation, and family prosperity.

05:00 AM - 10:00 PM Chandi Paath Rituals
5,000+ Years Ancient
व्यास गद्दी एवं वट वृक्ष (Vyas Gaddi)

Throne of Maharishi Ved Vyas

Beneath this majestic 5,000-year-old Akshaya Vat (Banyan tree), Maharishi Veda Vyas divided the single Veda into Rig, Sama, Yajur, and Atharva, and composed the 18 Maha Puranas and the Mahabharata.

06:00 AM - 08:00 PM Holy Banyan Tree
Swayambhu Vigraha
हनुमान गढ़ी (Hanuman Garhi)

Dakshin-Mukhi Lord Hanuman

A rare, south-facing self-manifested deity of Lord Hanuman carrying Lord Rama and Lakshmana on His sacred shoulders upon their glorious return from defeating Ahiravana in Patala Loka.

05:00 AM - 09:30 PM Sankat Mochan
Purana Discourse Seat
सूत गद्दी (Soot Gaddi)

Puranic Assembly of 88,000 Rishis

The venerated seat where disciple of Vyas, Romaharshana and Sage Sootji narrated the sacred Srimad Bhagavatam and Puranic wisdom to Kulapati Saunaka and the 88,000 rishis during their 1,000-year Mahayajna.

06:00 AM - 07:30 PM Purana Pravachan
Supreme Sacrifice
महर्षि दधीचि कुंड (Dadhichi Kund - Mishrikh)

The Crucible of Indomitable Will

Located 10 km from Chakra Tirtha, this is where Sage Dadhichi performed holy snan with water from all holy rivers and willingly donated his mortal bones to Lord Indra to forge the celestial weapon Vajra.

05:30 AM - 08:30 PM Sarva Tirtha Snan
Ramayana Era
दशाश्वमेध घाट - गोमती नदी

Lord Rama's Ashwamedha Ghat

The sanctified riverbank on the serene Gomti where Maryada Purushottam Lord Rama concluded His Ashwamedha Yajna under Sage Vashistha’s guidance, performing ten horse sacrifices to sanctify the realm.

Open 24 Hours Gomti Maha Aarti
Devata Pratishtha
देवदेवेश्वर मंदिर एवं सीता कुंड

Ancient Shivalinga & Mata Sita's Kund

One of the oldest Shiva shrines in Awadh consecrated by the Devatas before initiating penance. Adjacent is Sita Kund, sanctified by Mata Sita during her sacred stay in the hermitages of Naimisharanya.

05:00 AM - 09:00 PM Bilva Archana

Complete Travel Logistics

How to Reach Naimisharanya Dham

Located in Sitapur district of Uttar Pradesh, Naimisharanya (Neemsar) is exceptionally well-connected by expressways, railways, and international air hubs.

By Air (हवाई मार्ग)

Chaudhary Charan Singh International Airport, Lucknow (LKO)

  • Distance: 85 km (~2 hours drive via NH-24).
  • Direct flights from Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Dubai.
  • Prepaid taxis and car rentals readily available at terminal.

By Train (रेल मार्ग)

Balamau Jn, Sitapur Jn & Lucknow Charbagh

  • Balamau Junction (BLM): 30 km (Nearest major station on Delhi-Moradabad line).
  • Sitapur Junction (STP): 38 km (Direct connectivity).
  • Lucknow Charbagh (LKO/LJN): 85 km (National railway hub).

By Road (सड़क मार्ग)

Smooth 4-lane Highway & UPSRTC Buses

  • From Lucknow: Via Kaiserbagh / Sitapur Road (85 km).
  • From Delhi: Via Agra Expressway & Lucknow or Bareilly-Sitapur.
  • Frequent UPSRTC buses run every 30 minutes from Lucknow.

Essential Guidelines

Pilgrim Guidelines & Frequent Questions

Everything you need to know for an auspicious, respectful, and hassle-free pilgrimage to Naimisharanya Dham.

Chakra Tirtha Snan Etiquette

Take a holy dip with reverence. Enter through designated paved ghats. Chanting the Gayatri Mantra or Mahamrityunjay Mantra during snan enhances spiritual merit manifold.

Temple Dress Code

Modest traditional attire is recommended. For men: Dhoti-Kurta or traditional pants. For women: Sarees, Salwar Kameez or modest attire. Avoid leather items inside inner sanctums.

Best Season to Visit

October to March offers pleasant weather. The most spiritually potent times are Somvati Amavasya, Chaitra & Ashwin Navratri, and the Phalgun 84 Kosi Parikrama.
